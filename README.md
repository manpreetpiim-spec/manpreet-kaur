# Manpreet Kaur — Digital Marketing Portfolio

A responsive, single-page portfolio for a digital marketing specialist. Open `index.html` in a browser to preview it; the page uses plain HTML and CSS and does not need a build step.

## Page structure and copy

1. **Sticky header:** Name/wordmark, Home, About, Services, Case Studies, and a “Let’s chat” call to action.
2. **Hero:** “Hello. Let’s grow your brand online.” A discovery-led introduction, primary contact link, and portrait image.
3. **Positioning statement:** A short point of view on thoughtful, audience-focused marketing.
4. **Services:** SEO and content strategy, paid ads and Google Ads, social media growth, and email and campaign analytics.
5. **About:** Manpreet’s collaborative, strategy-first approach and what clients can expect.
6. **Selected work:** Two case-study layouts for explaining a client challenge, approach, and verified outcome.
7. **Testimonials:** Two quote cards with client attribution.
8. **Contact and footer:** Email call to action, quick links, and social profiles.

## Visual direction

- **Palette:** Cream `#f7f4ee`, warm beige `#eee9df`, charcoal `#292a26`, muted text `#6d6b63`, and terracotta `#a65f4b`.
- **Type:** DM Sans for navigation, body copy, and controls; Playfair Display for editorial headlines and selected display text. Google Fonts is loaded remotely; system fallbacks are included.
- **Layout:** A centered, 1160px maximum content width, generous section spacing, restrained borders, and a small number of warm, high-contrast calls to action.
- **Responsive behavior:** Side-by-side hero and about layouts on wide screens; service cards, case studies, testimonials, and navigation adapt for tablets and small screens. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

## Before publishing

- Replace the example portrait with Manpreet’s approved portrait. The current image is a remote Unsplash placeholder.
- Replace the sample case-study descriptions with real client names, challenges, and measured outcomes; do not publish unverified results.
- Replace both testimonial placeholders with genuine, permission-approved quotes and client attributions.
- Confirm `hello@manpreetkaur.com` is the right public contact address and update the email links if needed.
- Replace the LinkedIn and Instagram homepage links with Manpreet’s actual profile URLs. Add X or other profiles if relevant.
- Update the page title and meta description if the service positioning or target audience changes.

## Adapting to a site builder

Use each numbered section above as a page section in Squarespace or Webflow. Keep the desktop content width near 1160px, use a two-column hero with a portrait, and collapse it to one column on mobile. Use the palette and type pair above as global design tokens, then set section padding responsively rather than fixing heights. Keep the testimonials and case studies unpublished or clearly marked as drafts until verified client material is ready.
